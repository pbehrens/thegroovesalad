What's Down There

⛏️

Dig, Don't Steer

Spend your dig budget on tunnels and forks, wire triggers and pressure plates to the machinery, then let the worms do the rest. Worms have real body length and absolutely will not stop for each other.

🎯

Deterministic Simulation

The dashed preview runs the exact same simulation as the real thing, so what you see before Start is what happens after it. When a run goes wrong, fixing the plan is the fun part.

🗺️

50 Levels + a Daily Puzzle

50 hand-made levels that teach one trick at a time, then combine them all — plus a daily puzzle chosen by your local date. No streaks, no reminders, just today's dirt.

🔗

Share Codes

Solved a level your way? Copy a short code and send it to a friend, who pastes it and watches your build run. Works offline, no account needed.
